## 版本 A

**main_axis.headline**:You move under steady outside pressure, and you answer it by quietly producing solid work rather than by fighting back.

**main_axis.body**:Two forces run jointly at the front: Companion — your own judgment and the peers who work alongside you — and Eating God — output that flows naturally, craft and enjoyment. Behind them stand Direct Resource (systems, credentials, and backing) and Seven Killings (external pressure and hard demands). Barely loaded at the back are Rob Wealth, Hurting Officer, and Indirect Wealth: the reflex to compete head-on for resources, the urge to publicly challenge rules, and the nose for spotting opportunities stay largely unused.

**main_axis.dominant**:Companion, Eating God (jointly heaviest)

**main_axis.secondary**:Direct Resource, Seven Killings

**main_axis.latent**:Rob Wealth, Hurting Officer, Indirect Wealth

**main_axis.evidence**:Companion and Eating God are jointly heaviest — Eating God weighs 9 (month stem Xin, plus hidden Xin in the day branch You and in the year branch Chou), Companion weighs 8 (hour stem Ji and year-branch hidden Ji, plus hidden Wu in the month and hour branches Si). Direct Resource weighs 6, from hidden Bing in both Si branches. Seven Killings weighs 5, the year stem Yi. Rob Wealth 2, Hurting Officer 2, Indirect Wealth 1; Direct Wealth, Direct Officer, and Indirect Resource are all zero.

**core_loop.headline**:Pressure lands on you → you absorb it and hand back finished work → recognition tightens the pressure again.

**core_loop.from**:Seven Killings

**core_loop.to**:Eating God

**core_loop.flow**:When a hard demand comes down — a deadline, a standard someone else set — you do not argue with it; you go quiet, sit down, and let the finished thing speak for you. The better the work, the more is asked of you next, and the loop tightens. Meanwhile your own judgment keeps agreeing to take more on, because peers and self-reliance both tell you that you can handle it.

**core_loop.driver**:Day Master Ji earth is strong; the engine lists metal, water, and wood as favorable. With a strong self, Companion weight (8) is self-replication that adds load, while the heavy Eating God (9) is the healthy drain — output is how this chart releases pressure. Seven Killings (5) supplies the push; Direct Resource (6) buffers it. The Wealth family carries almost nothing (Indirect Wealth 1, Direct Wealth 0), so the loop ends at output and never continues into results or reward.

**core_loop.leak**:The cost sits at the far end of the loop: you convert pressure into polished work again and again, but almost none of it turns into money, position, or anything banked for yourself — water, the element that carries Wealth for you, is missing from all eight visible characters, and Direct Wealth carries zero weight.
This eases in the ten years starting around age 42 (the Bing Zi pillar), where water finally runs in the branch, and again in the ten years starting around age 52 (the Yi Hai pillar); in those stretches, conversations about price, terms, and what the work is worth stop feeling foreign.
Until then, build the habit yourself: before accepting the next hard demand, write down what you get out of it beyond the work being done — a rate, a title, a named credit — and treat 'nothing' as a reason to negotiate, not a reason to refuse the work.

**core_loop.evidence**:Age-42 stretch: Bing Zi pillar; age-52 stretch: Yi Hai pillar — both branches are water, the element absent from the chart's visible count (water 0). Officer-Killings pressure: year stem Yi as Seven Killings. Absorption via craft: Eating God 9 across month stem and hidden stems of Chou, You, Si; Direct Resource 6 from hidden Bing in the two Si branches. Self-replication: Companion 8 from hour stem Ji and hidden earth stems; the Day Master is strong, so these add load rather than help.

**structure_type.name**:the quiet craftsman who works down pressure

**structure_type.one_line**:You run as a self-reliant maker: pressure comes in, craft goes out, and the same pattern repeats without needing an audience. The design keeps you steadily productive but keeps the rewards side small, because the step from finished work to personal gain is the one you skip.

**capability_source.headline**:Strongest when a hard, concrete demand must be met with careful, finished work; weakest when the task is to price, sell, or compete openly for resources.

**capability_source.text**:Your strength comes from a strong self backed by craft and a quiet system of knowledge: while the others on the team are still discussing how to approach the client's strict spec, you have already produced a working version and refined it twice by evening. The same structure that makes this fast is why the pricing conversation at the end of the project feels like the hardest part of the job.

**capability_source.evidence**:Day Master Ji earth is strong, so producing output drains it healthily; Eating God 9 and Direct Resource 6 support sustained, careful work under Seven Killings (5) pressure. The weak scene traces to the Wealth family at weight 1 (Indirect Wealth 1, Direct Wealth 0), Rob Wealth only 2, and water absent from all eight visible characters.

**structure_fingerprint**:Absorbs pressure, answers with finished craft, repeats; converts little of the work into money or standing for himself.

## 版本 B

**main_axis.headline**:You are pushed by the need to make things and say what you think, and that output is what carries you.

**main_axis.body**:The leading force is Eating God — output that flows naturally, craft and enjoyment; it is jointly heaviest with Companion, your own judgment and peers who move with you. Next comes Direct Resource — systems, credentials, and backing — which gives you something solid to draw on. Sitting unused is Indirect Wealth — a nose for opportunities, mobilizing resources — present only in hidden stems and thin.

**main_axis.dominant**:Eating God, Companion (jointly heaviest)

**main_axis.secondary**:Direct Resource

**main_axis.latent**:Indirect Wealth

**main_axis.evidence**:Eating God and Companion are jointly heaviest (9 and 8): the month stem is Eating God, the day branch is Eating God, the hour stem is Companion, and the year branch hidden stems include Companion and Eating God. Direct Resource weighs 6, carried by the month and hour branch hidden stems. Indirect Wealth weighs 1, appearing only in the year branch hidden stems.

**core_loop.headline**:You make things and speak your mind, then lean on what you already know, then make more.

**core_loop.from**:Eating God

**core_loop.to**:Direct Resource

**core_loop.flow**:You start by producing — a draft, a fix, a straight opinion — and that output is how you engage with work and people. When the output runs into a wall, you fall back on what you have already learned or on the backing you have secured, and that base lets you produce again. The loop turns smoothly as long as the base keeps growing; when it does not, you keep producing from the same store.

**core_loop.driver**:The Day Master is slightly strong (偏旺), so output and wealth are the favorable directions; Resource and Companion add weight rather than relieve it. Eating God at 9 and Companion at 8 dominate, with Direct Resource at 6 feeding the base.

**core_loop.leak**:The cost is that you keep drawing on the same store of knowledge and backing without topping it up, so the work starts to repeat itself and the range of what you can produce stops widening.
In the ten years starting around age 42 (the Bing Zi pillar), water runs stronger and the base gets a chance to deepen, which makes it easier to learn something new instead of recycling what you know.
For now, set aside a fixed block each week to study something outside your current toolkit, and finish one small piece of output from it before the week ends.

**core_loop.evidence**:Day Master is slightly strong (偏旺), favoring output, wealth, and officer-killings. Eating God weighs 9, Companion 8, Direct Resource 6, Seven Killings 5, Hurting Officer 2, Rob Wealth 2, Indirect Wealth 1. The ten years starting around age 42 are the Bing Zi pillar (丙子), where water appears in the branch.

**structure_type.name**:the maker who leans on what he already knows

**structure_type.one_line**:You run on producing and speaking, and you steady yourself with accumulated knowledge and backing; the structure works when that base keeps growing.

**capability_source.headline**:Strongest when you must turn a rough idea into something finished and explain it out loud; weakest when you must keep producing from the same store without new input.

**capability_source.text**:Your strength comes from the combination of Eating God and Companion: you generate output quickly and you are comfortable acting on your own judgment alongside peers. In a meeting where the brief is vague, you are the one who sketches the first version and talks the room through it while others are still asking clarifying questions. Direct Resource gives you a base to draw on, so the output has something behind it rather than being empty talk.

**capability_source.evidence**:Eating God weighs 9 (month stem, day branch, year branch hidden stem), Companion weighs 8 (hour stem, year branch hidden stem), Direct Resource weighs 6 (month and hour branch hidden stems). Day Master is slightly strong, so output and self-direction carry the load.

**structure_fingerprint**:Produces and speaks first, steadies with accumulated knowledge, and needs new input to keep the output from repeating.
