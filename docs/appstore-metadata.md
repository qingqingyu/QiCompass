## en(English)

### Name
QiCompass: Bazi Reading(23 / 30)

### Subtitle
Your chart, read like a book(28 / 30)

### Keywords
four pillars,chinese astrology,compatibility,daily horoscope,wuxing,heavenly stems,daily fortune(96 / 100)

### Description

QiCompass is a Bazi reading app that takes charting seriously. Enter a birth time and place; the chart is built with true solar time correction and fully deterministic rules — the same input always produces the same chart.

**A book that belongs to you**
Pillars, elements, Ten Gods, symbolic stars, and luck periods, laid out chapter by chapter in the style of a classical Chinese almanac. The first two chapters are free: your chart's main structure — what drives it, where it loops — and your talents: what comes naturally, and what quietly drains you. Later chapters are a one-time purchase tied to this chart — they never expire.

**Today's page**
One page each day: what suits you, what to avoid, and the hours that matter, with a daily reading and illustration.

**Two charts, side by side**
Enter two people's birth details and see the rhythm of the relationship — where you complement each other, where you clash. The first two chapters are free; the full assessment is a one-time purchase.

**Ask a question**
Wondering about something today? Just ask. New installs include 3 free questions; additional packs available.

All charting comes from deterministic rules — AI is used only to phrase the reading well. We make no promises about outcomes and attach no rigid labels: we show the tendencies your chart suggests, for your reference.

For entertainment purposes only.
