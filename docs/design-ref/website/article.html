壹What a Jia Wood Day Master describes

In BaZi, your Day Master is the stem of the day you were born. It's the reference point: every other character in the chart is read as support, output, pressure or resource for it.

Jia is the first of the ten Heavenly Stems and the Yang form of Wood. Classical texts picture it as a great tree: rooted, upright, growing in one direction for a long time. Where Yi Wood bends and climbs, Jia stands.

贰Strengths and growth edges

These are tendencies the chart describes, not fixed traits. The rest of your chart can strengthen or soften every line below.

Tends toward 长

  • Steady, long-range effort
  • Principled; keeps their word
  • Protective of people under their "canopy"
  • Comfortable leading from the front

Watch for 短

  • Rigidity when plans change
  • Taking on weight others should carry
  • Slow to ask for help
  • Pride that hears advice as criticism

肆Strong vs. weak Jia Wood

"Strong" isn't better. It describes whether the Day Master has more support than pressure. A strong Jia usually benefits from outlets — Fire (expression) and Metal (structure). A weak Jia usually benefits from Water (resource) and Wood (peers). Strength is judged from season, roots in the branches, and visible support — here's the full method.

陆Work and study tendencies

Jia tends to do well where growth is slow and visible: building something over years, teaching, planning, roles with a clear line of responsibility. It tends to struggle where direction changes weekly. Which kind of work suits you depends on which Ten Gods are strong in your chart — an Eating God-heavy Jia and an Officer-heavy Jia look very different at work.

柒Reading Jia in your own chart

  • Check the month branch. It sets the season — see the table above.
  • Look for roots. Wood hidden in the branches (寅 卯 亥 辰 未) gives Jia somewhere to stand.
  • Count support vs. pressure. Water and Wood support; Fire, Earth and Metal spend or press.
  • Then read the Ten Gods — they say how that energy shows up.

捌FAQ

Is Jia Wood a good Day Master?
No Day Master is better or worse. Balance in the whole chart matters more than which stem you have.
Which Day Master combines with Jia Wood?
Ji Earth. Jia and Ji form one of the five stem combinations (甲己合).
How do I know if I'm a Jia Wood Day Master?
You need your birth date; the time only matters if you were born near 23:00. Use the Day Master finder.
