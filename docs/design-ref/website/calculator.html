Method

How this calculator works

True Solar Time 真太阳时

We convert clock time to local solar time from your birthplace's longitude and the equation of time, then pick the hour pillar. Historical time zones and daylight saving are applied first. Read more

The 23:00 day boundary 子初换日

The Chinese day begins at the start of the Rat hour, 23:00. A birth at 23:30 belongs to the next day's pillar. Some calculators split "early" and "late" Rat hours; we don't. Read more

Solar-term months 节气定月

The month pillar changes on the 12 "jie" solar terms, not on the lunar month or the 1st. A birth on 3 February may still belong to the previous year's pillar.

Sources 出处

Calendar conversion by an open-source engine tested against published reference charts. Symbolic stars follow one fixed table from Sanming Tonghui (三命通会). We never mix schools on one chart.

Calculator questions

壹Why is my hour pillar different on another site?
Most often True Solar Time or the 23:00 rule. If you were born within ~30 minutes of an odd hour (23:00, 01:00, 03:00…), check both.
贰What if I only know roughly when I was born?
Tick "I don't know my birth time". Your Day Master and six of eight characters stay certain. What still works.
叁Why do you ask for sex at birth?
Traditional rules use it only to set whether luck pillars run forward or backward. It doesn't change the four pillars.
