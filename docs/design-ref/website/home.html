壹The basics

What is BaZi?

BaZi (八字, "eight characters") describes the moment you were born as four pillars — year, month, day and hour. Each pillar holds a Heavenly Stem and an Earthly Branch, giving eight characters that show how the Five Elements are balanced in your chart.

The day pillar's stem is your Day Master — the reference point the rest of the chart is read against. The other seven characters relate to it as one of the Ten Gods.

壹Is this BaZi calculator free?

贰Do I need my exact birth time?
